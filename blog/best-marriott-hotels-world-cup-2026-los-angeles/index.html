Los Angeles SoFi Stadium · Inglewood Bonvoy Ambassador Pick

Most World Cup guides for LA will point you straight at SoFi Stadium and call it done. I've stayed at the Marina del Rey Marriott, and the best Marriott experience in LA isn't always the most obvious one. The M Lounge there had the best lounge menu I've encountered across all my Bonvoy stays — a genuine standout. And the walk to Venice Beach gives you a side of LA that downtown properties simply can't match. Here's how I'd break down the city for World Cup 2026.

How LA Works

LA is a car city — that one fact changes everything about how you choose a hotel for World Cup 2026. SoFi Stadium is in Inglewood, southwest of downtown. The JW Marriott at LA Live is walking distance on game day. But the Marina del Rey Marriott puts you 10–15 minutes from the stadium while giving you Venice Beach, the boardwalk, and the best M Lounge menu I've had anywhere in the Bonvoy portfolio. Both choices are legitimate — it depends on what you want from LA.

LA inventory moves fast for big events

SoFi hosts multiple group stage matches. The Inglewood and Downtown properties fill first. Marina del Rey moves slower — but it won't last.

The Hotels — Ranked

🏟️ Best for SoFi Stadium

JW Marriott Los Angeles L.A. LIVE

The strategic pick for anyone who wants to minimize travel friction on match days. The JW is part of the LA Live complex — walking distance to SoFi Stadium. When 70,000 people are trying to get back from a game, being on foot while everyone else is in traffic is a meaningful advantage. Strong Ambassador recognition, solid F&B, and the energy of LA Live on its doorstep.

Category 6 — this will be the most in-demand property in LA on match days. Book early.

🌊 Best Lifestyle Pick — Personal Stay

Marina del Rey Marriott

Personal Stay

This is where I'd stay, and where I have stayed. Walking distance to Venice Beach and the canals — a version of LA that downtown properties can't offer. The M Lounge here had the best lounge menu I've encountered across my Bonvoy stays. The staff were exceptional across every interaction. For World Cup you're 10–15 min from SoFi by ride share, with a quieter and far more livable base than the downtown cluster.

💎 Best Luxury

The Ritz-Carlton Los Angeles

Within the same LA Live complex as the JW — the luxury upgrade for fans who want proximity to the stadium without compromising on finish. Same walking distance to SoFi, higher category, full Ritz-Carlton service. For Ambassador members with Suite Night Awards, the RC gives you the best shot at a genuine suite on a World Cup night.

🌆 Best Hollywood Base

W Hollywood

For fans who want the entertainment district — Hollywood Boulevard, walkable dining, the cultural energy of LA's best-known neighbourhood. Stadium access requires a ride, but for fans who want to experience LA as much as the matches, the tradeoff is worth it. Go for the location and the vibe.

Trip Tips

🚗

Getting to SoFi

Ride share from most properties. Budget for surge pricing on match days. From Marina del Rey: ~10–15 min. If you're at LA Live, you're walking. Metro K Line is the underrated option — direct to SoFi.

📅

Book JW LA Live Early

The most in-demand property in LA on game days. Group stage matches at SoFi will clear inventory quickly. Lock in a refundable rate now — optimise rate type later.

🎖️

Points Strategy

Marina del Rey is lower category — strong points value on a property I'd genuinely recommend. Suite Night Awards at the Ritz-Carlton for match nights give you the best upgrade shot.

LA is Group Stage Gold

The beach or the stadium — you don't have to choose

Marina del Rey gives you Venice Beach in the morning and SoFi Stadium in the afternoon. That's a World Cup trip. Book your base before it disappears.
