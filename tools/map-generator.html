How do I get a GPX file?
StravaOn strava.com, open your ride, click the ··· button on the left, then Export GPX. (The phone app can't export.)
Garmin ConnectOn connect.garmin.com, open the activity, click the ⚙ gear at the top right, then Export to GPX.
Komoot, Wahoo, othersOpen the tour or ride and look for Export or Download GPX in its menu.
