About the Journal

This journal started as personal notes from the road — hotels that surprised, passes that humbled, and places that refused to leave. It's written by a motorcycle traveler and adventure junkie who moves through the world on two wheels as much as by plane. KTM rider. Alpine pass collector. No press trips. No gifted stays. Just an honest record of the roads and rooms worth remembering.
