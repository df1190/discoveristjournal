The Verdicts

9.2

The Cambrian

The best all-round alpine stay in Switzerland at this price point. Design is confident without being cold. The ski-in access and spa make winter the obvious season.

9.0

The Hotel Lucerne

Jean Nouvel's building is reason enough to stay. The restaurant is the best of the three. Go for architecture and the lake — not a typical resort experience.

8.7

The Park Gstaad

Grand, famous, and expensive. Delivers on the classic Swiss luxury promise. Better for special occasions than everyday stays. The pool and spa are exceptional.

9.4

Vigilius Mountain Resort

One of Europe's finest mountain hotels. The cable car access, Matteo Thun design, and total commitment to silence make this unmissable. Best in January or October.
