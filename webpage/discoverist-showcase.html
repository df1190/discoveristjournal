BALI, INDONESIA
01

Bali on a Scooter

Southeast Asia — 14 Days

Chasing temple smoke through jungle mist. Waking up in a Marriott villa to rice terrace views, then renting a scooter and getting genuinely lost for six hours through Ubud's back roads. The kind of trip that resets your internal clock.

Moto Luxury Hotels Culture Marriott Bonvoy
02

Tokyo's Hidden Hours

East Asia — 10 Days

Arrived Star Alliance Gold, upgraded to a skyline suite, and spent every night between ramen alleys and late-night izakayas. Days were for shrines and electric motorcycles. Tokyo never fully sleeps, and neither did I.

City Food Star Alliance Night Photography
03

Alps Ridge Run

Europe — 8 Days

Renting a BMW GS in Zurich and navigating every mountain pass that wasn't closed by snow. Furka, Grimsel, Susten — the triptych of passes that every moto traveler has pinned on their bucket list. Everything earned.

Adventure Moto Mountain Passes BMW GS
04

Marrakech Medina

North Africa — 7 Days

Navigating a riad city that predates GPS, where every alley looked identical and every turn smelled of saffron and leather. Stayed at La Mamounia and photographed the souk at golden hour until my battery died.

Culture Photography Luxury Riad
