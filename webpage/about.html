Who I Am

Not a travel blogger.
A traveler who writes.

The Discoverist Journal started as a personal record — a way to document hotels, roads, and places that deserved more than a five-star rating and a one-liner. Over years of traveling through Europe, Asia, the Americas, and beyond, it became something more structured: a reference for people who travel the way I do.

I'm not a professional influencer. I pay for my own stays, or earn them through loyalty programs I've built over years of real travel. Marriott Bonvoy Ambassador. Star Alliance Gold. These aren't brand deals — they're the result of a life spent on the road.

Every review, every route recommendation, every gear note comes from personal experience. If I haven't been there, I won't write about it.

The Approach

Written From
the Road. Not an Office.

Travel content has a noise problem. Most of it is recycled, incentivised, or written by someone who spent two nights somewhere on a press trip. The Discoverist Journal exists to be the opposite of that.

It's slower. More considered. The kind of writing you come back to when you're actually planning something — not just scrolling.

01

Real Experience Only

Every hotel review, route guide, and destination post is based on a stay or journey I completed myself.

02

No Sponsored Fluff

Affiliate links help keep the journal running. But they never dictate what gets written or recommended.

03

Intention Over Volume

Fewer posts, written properly. This isn't a content farm. It's a journal.

04
