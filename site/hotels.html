🇮🇹 Lana, South Tyrol, Italy

Vigilius Mountain Resort

Spa Cable Car Access Moto Friendly Pass Proximity

Accessible only by cable car from Lana village, Vigilius sits at 1,500m with views across the Merano basin. The spa is the best in South Tyrol. Parking for bikes is in the valley — secure, covered, and 20 minutes from the Stelvio base. It's the right hotel after a day on the passes: quiet, considered, and slightly removed from the world below.

🇨🇭 Lucerne, Switzerland

The Hotel Lucerne

Secure Garage City Centre Rest Day Base

Lucerne is the best Swiss city for a rest day mid-tour. The Hotel has a secure underground garage and an excellent restaurant. Susten and Furka are 90 minutes out — stay two nights and use one day for the passes.

🇮🇹 Bormio, Italy

Bormio — Pass Base

Courtyard Parking Early Breakfast (6:30) Stelvio 20min

The operational base for Stelvio and Gavia. 6:30am breakfast means you're on the pass before 8am. Internal courtyard parking for bikes is secure and free. Bormio also has thermal baths — a solid option after a day on the Gavia.

🇨🇭 Andermatt, Switzerland

Andermatt — Pass Hub

Underground Parking Furka 15min Gotthard 10min

Andermatt is the best single base for Swiss pass riding. Furka in 15 minutes, Susten in 25, Gotthard in 10. Multiple hotels at all price points. The new Chedi development adds premium options. Underground heated parking at most properties.

🇳🇴 Flåm, Norway

Flåm — Fjord Base

Secure Parking Fjord Access Ferry Connections

The starting point for Norway's Atlantic Road and Sognefjord loop. Secure bike parking, fjord views, and ferry access for loop routing. Book 6–8 weeks in advance for July — this fills quickly. The Aurlandsfjord is visible from most rooms.

🇦🇹 Zell am See, Austria

Zell am See — Alpine Hub

Grossglockner 45min Lakeside Secure Parking

The natural base for the Grossglockner Hochalpenstrasse. The toll road starts 45 minutes from Zell am See. Lakeside location means you can ride in the morning and swim in the afternoon. Several hotels with covered bike parking.
